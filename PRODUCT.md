# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Arabic-speaking individuals who want to understand and manage their everyday finances quickly, without unnecessary complexity. The product also supports English and mirrored LTR layouts.

## Product Purpose

Masarifi gives people a clear, accurate picture of their money across accounts, transactions, reports, budgets, salary, obligations, and savings. Success means the user can understand their current position and recent movement within seconds, then act with confidence.

## Positioning

An Arabic-first personal-finance experience that brings manual and automatic transaction tracking, planning, reports, and privacy controls into one coherent view without sacrificing numerical precision.

## Operating Context

People check Masarifi briefly and repeatedly on mobile, often one-handed and while distracted. The Home screen is the primary orientation point: current balance, period movement, account scope, and recent activity must be immediately understandable.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing real financial data, routes, filters, account scope, privacy masking, and transaction behaviors.
- Financial amounts must never be visually truncated or separated from their currency.
- Arabic RTL and English LTR are equal first-class layouts.
- The application runs through Expo across Android and web, with existing iOS-compatible foundations.
- Redesign work must reuse the established Masarifi design tokens and accessible interaction patterns.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Masarifi / مصاريفي.
- Trust comes before decoration; the visual experience should feel simple, premium, and calm.
- Dark teal is an established brand anchor, but page composition may change materially.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current Home implementation and real seeded financial content.
- Existing Arabic and English localization.
- Existing Masarifi design tokens, icon system, financial meaning colors, and category visuals.
- Supplied screenshots serve as product and density references, not mandatory compositions.

## Product Principles

- Numerical truth is never abbreviated, hidden, or visually ambiguous.
- The first viewport explains the user's financial position within seconds.
- Complexity is progressively disclosed rather than shown all at once.
- Every layout remains usable with large text and mixed Arabic/Latin financial content.
- Privacy remains user-controlled and centrally consistent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support large text up to 200%, screen readers, clear focus order, minimum touch targets, non-color-only financial meaning, Arabic RTL, English LTR, and WCAG AA contrast where applicable.
