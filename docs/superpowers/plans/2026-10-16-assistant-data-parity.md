Financial conventions: ReportsRepository readSummary defines income as confirmed income amounts and spending as expense plus expense fee, minus refund amounts, plus fees on other kinds. Category breakdowns subtract linked refunds from the original category and exclude fees. Goal progress is opening tracked amount plus signed immutable movements. Obligation remaining and overdue use v_obligation_status. Account totals include active include_in_totals accounts only and cannot be exact when a contributing balance projection is missing.
