## Decisions the operator must confirm

1. Legal operator/controller name, entity type, registered address/number and actual legal domicile. Confirm operational handling of the user-supplied contact masarifi.ratibi@gmail.com; do not invent a registered entity.
2. UAE launch is confirmed. Broad age access remains conditional on a compliant age/guardian and child-safety design. Confirm the operator's regulatory status and applicable rules; Google identity is not an age/guardian verification process. Device timezone, Arabic UI and default country `SA` do not establish governing law.
3. The purpose-to-lawful-ground register, optional processing choices and prominent SMS/notification/AI disclosures. An acknowledgement of a privacy notice must not be used as blanket consent to every purpose. Determine whether separate optional choices are required; do not force optional processing as a condition of unrelated core use.
4. The actual production provider/model endpoints, account privacy/logging settings, processing/support countries, subprocessor agreements and transfer mechanisms. Include authentication, VPS, database/storage/backups, AI, push and the actual email provider if enabled. A restricted gateway request does not establish all contractual guarantees.
5. A category-specific retention schedule with durations or objective criteria, including local recovery journals, raw tracking imports, recordings, transcripts, conversations, support attachments, reports, exports, security/audit data, backups and legal holds. Confirm active deletion/retention workers and evidence of end-to-end completion before promising a deadline.
6. Accessible rights and deletion request channels, including a public web deletion-request resource if required by distribution policy. Confirm identity checks, response procedures, escalation/complaint contact and legally applicable deadlines. The draft does not invent a working website or mailbox.
7. Governing law/courts and any language precedence, lawful suspension/complaint procedure, consumer warranties and any liability allocation. No arbitration, monetary cap, indemnity or foreign jurisdiction was silently selected.
8. Whether future paid features will exist. Billing remains excluded/deferred; these drafts create no subscription, payment terms, renewal or refunds policy for an offer that is not active.

## Professional legal and operational verification before public release

Have UAE counsel determine which federal, relevant free-zone and sector-specific laws actually apply and review the final Arabic/English equivalence. Assess controller information, legal grounds and rights, financial/credit data classification, processors, required impact assessment, incident obligations, international transfers and any representative/DPO/registration requirements. Review the Child Digital Safety framework and age/capacity rules before offering child access. Saudi references below are prior background only, not the intended launch framework. Do not infer GDPR applicability merely from EU infrastructure.

Google Play review should cover accurate Data safety declarations, prominent disclosure before sensitive access, the SMS-permission permitted-use/declaration process, AI data disclosure and both in-app and external account-deletion pathways. These documents do not themselves qualify the app for an SMS permission exception or certify store compliance. Optional permissions and hidden features were not enabled during this work.

Before activating mandatory acceptance, the user must review and approve the final text and version. Then separately design and approve a server-verifiable consent record bound to the owner, document versions and time, the timing for Google account provisioning versus acceptance, handling of declines/new versus returning users, and any required renewed consent. Merely making the client checkbox required is insufficient. Protected backend policy/schema/service changes stay in the consolidated approval process. No historical acceptance should be backfilled or inferred.

These sources inform the review questions. They do not prove Masarifi's contracts, vendor configuration or compliance. Wording above is original drafting; it does not reproduce a template or vendor policy.
