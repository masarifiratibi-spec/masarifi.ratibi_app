- UAE Child Digital Safety, Federal Decree-Law26/2025: the official materials identify specific conditions for under-13 data processing, including explicit documented/verifiable caregiver consent, withdrawal and safeguards, and proportionate age verification tied to platform classification. Confirm implementing decisions and Masarifi's classification with counsel. ## Implementation gaps requiring resolution before child-facing release

The application currently has no verified age assurance, guardian relationship/authority verification, parental consent receipt/withdrawal workflow, child-facing notice or classification-specific child controls. Google sign-in, optional birthday and the inactive Terms checkbox do not supply those controls. A minor's SMS, notification history, financial records and microphone/AI data need a specific purpose, proportionality and provider assessment. Do not collect extra identity documents through email or add a guardian database merely to simulate compliance.

Proposed next design after professional review: determine permitted age bands and functions; minimize age evidence; verify guardian authorization where required; make withdrawal accessible; define child privacy defaults and safe feature/provider limits; document rights and harm-reporting procedures. Assess whether some features must remain unavailable to children until those safeguards exist. These are review-dependent requirements for T12 and affected existing tasks, not implemented capabilities or authority to activate a protected policy.

The adult flow is not newly blocked; no age cutoff, guardian registration, mandatory legal consent, account record or hosted policy has been activated. The general consent preview remains disabled and unchecked. Final legal and implementation approval are still needed before public release.
